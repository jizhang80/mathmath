Exponent laws: the one idea you need

MTH1W

This is the second step back this session — the last one allowed. Then back to your problem.

An exponential equation and a logarithmic equation say the same thing two ways:

23 = 8   ⇔   log2(8) = 3

The base stays the base. The exponent is what the logarithm gives you. So log2(A) = 3 tells you A = 23, never 32.

Relates to MCR3U expectations C1.1 and C2.1 — official expectation (Ontario).

When you multiply powers of the same base, the exponents add: 23 · 24 = 27. When you raise a power to a power, they multiply: (23)4 = 212.

Keep the base; work only on the exponents. Relates to MTH1W expectation B3.4 — official expectation (Ontario).
